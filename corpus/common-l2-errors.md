# Common second-language errors

These are the errors that survive fluency — they come from the shape of the
learner's first language, not from carelessness.

**"I am agree."** *Agree* is a verb, not an adjective: *I **agree**.* Same with
*I am understand* → *I understand*.

**Uncountable nouns given a plural.** *Information, advice, news, furniture,
equipment, luggage, research, feedback, knowledge, software, homework, progress,
money* have no plural and take no *a/an*: *some **information***, *a piece of
**advice***, *much **research***, not *informations, advices, researches*.

**"since three years."** *Since* takes a point in time, *for* takes a duration,
and the verb is present perfect: *I have lived here **for** three years.*

**Present simple for something happening now.** *I **am working** on it right
now*, not *I work on it right now*. Conversely, stative verbs stay simple: *I
**want** it*, not *I am wanting it*.

**Double comparatives and superlatives.** *More easier, most fastest* → *easier,
fastest*. One-syllable adjectives take *-er/-est*; three-syllable adjectives
take *more/most*; two-syllable adjectives vary.

**Missing subject or dummy *it*.** English never drops the subject: *Is raining*
→ ***It** is raining.* *Is important to try* → ***It** is important to try.*

**Wrong verb pattern after another verb.** *enjoy, avoid, finish, suggest,
mind, practise, consider, recommend* + **-ing**: *I enjoy **reading**.*
*want, need, decide, hope, promise, agree, offer, refuse, manage* + **to**:
*I decided **to leave**.* *Make/let* + bare infinitive: *She made me **wait**.*

**Adjective order.** Opinion → size → age → shape → colour → origin → material →
purpose: *a lovely small old round brown Italian wooden salad bowl.*

**Question and negative formation.** Keep the auxiliary and use the base verb:
*Did you **go**?* not *Did you went?* *He doesn't **know*** not *He doesn't
knows.* Indirect questions revert to statement order: *Can you tell me where
**the station is**?* not *where is the station*.

**False friends and register.** *Actually* ≠ *currently*; *eventually* ≠
*possibly*; *sympathetic* ≠ *nice*; *sensible* ≠ *sensitive*.
