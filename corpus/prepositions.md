# Prepositions

## Time: in, on, at

- **at** — clock times, festivals, *at night, at the weekend* (BrE) /
  *on the weekend* (AmE): *at 6 p.m., at Christmas.*
- **on** — days and dates: *on Monday, on 3 June, on my birthday, on Monday
  morning.*
- **in** — longer periods: *in July, in 2019, in the summer, in the morning,
  in two hours* (= after two hours).

No preposition before *next, last, this, every*: *I saw her **last** Friday*,
not *on last Friday*.

## Place: in, on, at

- **in** — inside a space or area: *in the room, in London, in a car.*
- **on** — a surface or a line: *on the wall, on the second floor, on the bus,
  on the river.*
- **at** — a point or an event: *at the door, at the bus stop, at work, at the
  party, at 15 Main Street.*

*arrive **in** a country or city, arrive **at** a building or event* — never
*arrive to*.

## Dependent prepositions worth memorising

*depend **on**, listen **to**, wait **for**, look **for** (search) / **at**
(direct eyes) / **after** (take care of), belong **to**, consist **of**, result
**in** (cause) / **from** (be caused by), participate **in**, apologise **to**
someone **for** something, explain something **to** someone, married **to**,
interested **in**, good **at**, afraid **of**, responsible **for**, similar
**to**, different **from**, agree **with** a person / **to** a proposal /
**on** a plan.*

## Verbs that take no preposition

*discuss, enter, marry, phone, answer, approach, lack, resemble* are transitive:
*We discussed the problem* (not *discussed about*), *She entered the room* (not
*entered into*), *Phone me* (not *phone to me*).
